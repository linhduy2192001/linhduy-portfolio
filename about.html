About Hi, I'm Duy

Some inspiring words to describe yourself

If you catch someone running while holding a camera or fussing over a studio setup to review the latest tech—that’s probably me! I started as an IT student at TDTU, but my heart 'fell' for the art of storytelling. My two passion projects, 'Duy Mê Đạp' and 'Linh Duy Đây', are where I truly come alive. To me, it’s not just about making videos; it’s about sharing my journey of conquering roads and exploring tech in the most authentic way possible.

How I Got Here

It all started back in 2024. Long before I hit the open roads, I was already obsessed with the art of cinematography and tech reviews. As an IT student at TDTU, I spent countless hours mastering camera angles and editing techniques to create 'Linh Duy Đây'. Later on, I brought that same creative energy to the cycling world with 'Duy Mê Đạp'. For me, whether it’s a high-tech gadget or a long bike ride, it’s all about capturing the perfect story.
